## Frequently Asked Questions

Do I really need a Will if I’m still young and healthy?

Yes. Having a Will ensures your children, spouse, and assets are protected if the unexpected happens. Without one, Ontario’s intestacy laws decide who inherits, which may not reflect your wishes.

What happens to my children if I don’t name a guardian in my Will?

If no guardian is legally appointed, the courts decide who cares for your children. A Will lets you choose a trusted guardian and prevents family disputes.

Can my spouse automatically make decisions for me if I become incapacitated?

Not necessarily. Without a Power of Attorney, your spouse may need court approval. Setting up POAs ensures your spouse or chosen person can manage finances and healthcare seamlessly.
