## Our Practice Areas

## [Post-Graduate Work Permit (PGWP)](https://libertasimmigration.ca/visit-canada/)

-   Open work permit for graduates of eligible Canadian institutions
-   Valid for up to 3 years based on program length
-   Pathway to gaining Canadian work experience for PR

## [Study Permit](https://libertasimmigration.ca/study-in-canada/)

-   [Study Permit](https://libertasimmigration.ca/study-permit-in-canada/)
-   [Student Direct Stream](https://libertasimmigration.ca/student-direct-streamsds/)

## [Work Visa](https://libertasimmigration.ca/work-in-canada/)

-   Work Permit (Temporary Foreign Worker)
-   LMIA (Labour Market Impact Assessment)
-   PGWP (Post Graduate Work Permit)

## [Provincial Nominee Program (PNP)](https://libertasimmigration.ca/family-sponsorship-in-canada/)

-   [Province-specific streams tailored to local labour market needs](https://libertasimmigration.ca/spousal-sponsorship-in-canada/)
-   [Enhanced nomination adds 600 points to Express Entry profile](https://libertasimmigration.ca/parent-and-grandparent-sponsorship-in-canada/)
-   [Available for skilled workers, graduates, and entrepreneurs](https://libertasimmigration.ca/parent-and-grandparent-sponsorship-in-canada/)

## [Express Entry](https://libertasimmigration.ca/work-in-canada/)

-   Federal points-based system for skilled worker immigration
-   Manages applications for FSWP, FSTP, and CEC
-   Competitive draws held regularly based on CRS score

## [Visitor Visa / Super Visa](https://libertasimmigration.ca/family-sponsorship-in-canada/)

-   Temporary authorization to visit Canada for tourism or family
-   Super Visa allows parents and grandparents to stay up to 5 years per visit
-   Multiple-entry options available for frequent travellers

## [Parental Sponsorship](https://libertasimmigration.ca/work-in-canada/)

-   Canadian citizens and PRs can sponsor parents and grandparents
-   Includes Super Visa as a faster alternative option
-   Grants permanent residency upon successful approval

## [Spousal Sponsorship](https://libertasimmigration.ca/family-sponsorship-in-canada/)

-   Sponsor your spouse, common-law, or conjugal partner for PR
-   Inland and outland application streams available
-   Open work permit available while application is in progress

## [Citizenship](https://libertasimmigration.ca/work-in-canada/)

-   Apply after meeting physical presence and language requirements
-   Requires passing the Canadian citizenship test
-   Grants the right to a Canadian passport and full civic rights
