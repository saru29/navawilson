## What is Civil Litigation?

Civil litigation refers to legal disputes between individuals where criminal charges are not involved. Whenever two or more parties engage in a non-criminal legal dispute, it is categorized as civil litigation. Typically, in civil litigation, the parties are suing to enforce or to defend a legal right, and the suing party (the Plaintiff) is seeking to be compensated with either money damages or some action (specific performance.) A commercial dispute arises when at least one of the involved parties is a business entity, such as a partnership or corporation.

A commercial litigation lawyer is specifically educated, trained, and experienced in litigation aimed at businesses and business-related litigation. Such a lawyer’s role will imply various specific responsibilities depending on whether they are tasked with representing a commercial client or an individual in such a case.
