## Refinancing a Property in Ontario

Nava Wilson real estate lawyers collaborate with both individual and business clients to secure and facilitate financing for commercial and residential properties. By conducting thorough document reviews and meticulous preparation, we guarantee the protection of our clients’ interests throughout the entire process.

When refinancing or securing a mortgage on a property, the firm will handle the following:

-   Complete necessary searches, including title searches and PPSA searches;
-   Arrange to payout balances owing on any existing financing to be paid from the proceeds;

-   Prepare and review all documentation;
-   Register the mortgage on title.
