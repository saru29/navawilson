## Frequently Asked Questions

Why do high net-worth individuals need specialized estate planning?

Larger estates often involve multiple properties, businesses, or investments. Tailored estate planning helps minimize taxes, protect wealth, and ensure smooth succession.

Can estate planning help with business succession?

Yes. Business succession planning ensures your company continues smoothly under chosen leadership while protecting its value for your family or beneficiaries.
