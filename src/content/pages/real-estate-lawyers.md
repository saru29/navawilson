## [Buying a Property](/buying-a-property/)

Our team ensures the transfer of ownership and clear title to the property are provided to the buyer on closing.

During the transaction, the purchaser’s solicitor has the following duties:

-   Review the Agreement of Purchase and Sale and advise the Buyer of any potential issues.
-   Carry out necessary title searches to ensure that a clear title can be obtained on closing;
-   Submit title requisitions to the vendor’s solicitor;
-   Review responses to requisitions and address any deficiencies;
-   On receipt of mortgage instructions, prepare and execute closing documentation with the purchaser;
-   On the day of the closing, transfer closing funds to the vendor’s solicitor and complete title registrations in accordance with client instructions;
-   Report to the bank and the client;

## The Role of a Real Estate Lawyer in Ontario

When choosing a lawyer for your real estate needs in Ontario, it’s crucial to pick an experienced one. Experienced real estate lawyers understand the complexities that can arise during home sales and work diligently to protect your interests.

In Ontario, lawyers handle residential and commercial transactions. We review documents, conduct due diligence searches, prepare closing paperwork, and ensure the transaction aligns with the Agreement of Purchase and Sale.

For residential real estate, we assist with buying, selling, leasing, renting, and property disputes. We can also provide guidance on property transfers and family or inherited properties.

In commercial transactions, we advise on zoning issues, perform due diligence, and review lease contracts, focusing on the various steps involved in real estate deals.

A qualified Ontario real estate lawyer simplifies the process, saving you time and money. In Ontario, you can’t buy or sell property without a real estate lawyer because they are the only ones authorized to register transactions through “Teraview,” the government’s land records database. Lawyers are essential for all real estate transactions in the province.

What does a real estate lawyer do in Ontario?

A real estate lawyer in Ontario assists with legal aspects of property transactions, ensuring compliance with laws and regulations.  They review contracts, conduct searches, address title issues, ensure funds are securely transferred between buyers and sellers and facilitate the closing process.

What are the typical responsibilities of a real estate lawyer in Ontario?

They review contracts, conduct searches, address title issues, ensure funds are securely transferred between buyers and sellers and facilitate the closing process.

How much does a real estate lawyer in Ontario charge for their services?

Can a real estate lawyer help with residential and commercial properties in Ontario?

Yes, real estate lawyers can handle both residential and commercial transactions.  Some lawyers will specialize in either residential or commercial real estate.

What is the role of a real estate lawyer in Ontario during the closing process?

What should I look for when choosing a real estate lawyer in Ontario?

Look for experience, a clear fee structure, and good communication skills.

Can a real estate lawyer help with financing options in Ontario?

What happens if there are issues with the property title in Ontario?
